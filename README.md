Welcome to my record shop! I’m excited to present my third and final capstone, which consists of two different applications: a web-based app and a user API.

In my record shop, you can explore different categories, set your minimum and maximum price, and choose between CDs, vinyl records, and music accessories. Users can also log in, add as many items as they like to their shopping cart, and view the cart before making a purchase.
